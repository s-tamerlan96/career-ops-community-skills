# Quality checks

Apply the checks relevant to the requested task. Do not add unsupported facts to make an output appear complete.

## Vacancy fit and writing

- Compare the role with the user's confirmed requirements before drafting an application or external reply.
- State material conflicts and unresolved requirements clearly. Do not soften a hard mismatch into implied acceptance.
- Trace achievements and metrics to approved sources. Explain the concrete contribution and result without inflating ownership or causality.
- Match the requested language and audience. Voice samples guide style, not factual claims.

## Interview preparation and review

- Use the actual role and available interviewer context, and distinguish real questions from generated practice questions.
- Confirm speaker ownership before evaluating dialogue. Preserve ambiguities when the source cannot resolve them.
- When a full transcript review is requested, produce the cleaned substantive transcript, actual questions, answer comparison, and analysis required by the review skill.
- Keep actual answers separate from stronger answer patterns. Mark hypotheses and unknowns explicitly.
- Use real interview questions as stronger evidence of what was asked than mock questions; do not treat frequency in a small sample as a population estimate.
- Keep case-specific findings with the case, and sanitize reusable material before transferring it into shared resources.

## CV editing

- Compare the edited artifact with the intended source and requested scope.
- Verify that new text appeared, adjacent content remained intact, and no text overlaps, spills, or loses intended emphasis.
- Check line breaks, short metrics, section boundaries, and links in the final rendered artifact.
- If a preview cannot be reviewed reliably, provide exact text changes and state the limitation; do not claim visual verification.

## Handoffs and delivery

- Check that referenced files exist and that stage, outcome, and next action are consistent across the manifest, handoff, and tracker.
- Confirm the final artifact and its access mode before reporting completion. Local preview, saved draft, and public publication are different states.
- Report which checks ran and what remains unverified. Do not present a static file check as proof that a connector, save, or delivery succeeded.
