# CV adaptation flow

1. Identify the requested artifact and scope: suggestions, a copy, or edits to an existing CV. Preserve the user's chosen source and design unless changes are requested.
2. Extract the role's requirements and map them to approved evidence. Distinguish direct experience from transferable experience and unknowns.
3. Draft precise replacements. Preserve strong supported claims, truthful responsibility boundaries, and approximately the available text space. Explain the concrete function behind labels such as automation or AI assistance only when supported.
4. Use narrow edits where possible. Preserve rich-text emphasis, section boundaries, and layout; do not resize, reposition, or redesign beyond the request.
5. Inspect the changed and adjacent content in the final render. Check overflow, overlap, wrapping, links, and typography. If an editing tool merges regions or damages formatting, recover the source before continuing.
6. Follow `memory/permissions.md` for review, save, and outbound action. A saved CV is not a submitted application.
7. Report the final artifact only after verifying its state. If the user takes over editing, stop concurrent modifications and support their review.
