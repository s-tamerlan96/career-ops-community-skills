## Community skills: evidence, continuity, and process learning

These additional workflows extend the upstream career-ops rules. The upstream data contract, factual source boundary, application review requirement, and tracker integrity rules still apply.

- Before repeated, risky, or quality-sensitive work, read `memory/rules.md` and `memory/errors.md` when present. Read the relevant `memory/source_map.md`, `memory/quality_bar.md`, `memory/permissions.md`, or `memory/cv_flow.md` for the task.
- Candidate facts and positioning belong in approved user-layer sources. Agent mistakes and workflow controls belong in `memory/`. Neither process memory nor unrelated repositories may supply new candidate achievements or authorship claims.
- Separate confirmed facts, sourced observations, interpretations, hypotheses, and unknowns. Keep source type and confidence visible; a recap is not a transcript, and a draft is not a sent message.
- Preserve the user's confirmed responsibilities and the limits of their ownership. Do not replace a mixed role with a generic job label or infer authority from a job title.
- Resume a career case from its manifest and handoff when available. Check referenced evidence, unresolved conflicts, communication status, and next action before proceeding. When an outcome becomes known, reconcile the case and tracker and record the learning without inventing the reason for that outcome.
- Drafting does not authorize sending. Follow `memory/permissions.md` before submitting an application, sending a message, or publishing candidate information.
- If the user identifies an agent mistake, record a concise, factual entry in `memory/errors.md`. Do not copy unnecessary personal details or raw private conversations into the error record. Do not turn a hypothesis into an established mistake.
- When asked for `error-review` or to turn mistakes into durable rules, read `.agents/skills/error-review/SKILL.md`. Propose changes first; apply durable controls and archive processed entries only after the user's confirmation.
- Skill availability does not prove a connector is installed or authorized. Use supported local artifacts when possible and report missing access honestly.
