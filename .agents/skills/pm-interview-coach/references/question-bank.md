# PM Interview Question Bank

Use this bank only after checking real questions and opportunity-specific materials. Select questions that test distinct competencies. Adapt nouns and constraints to the target product without changing the competency being tested.

## HR and Motivation

- Walk me through your background for this role.
- Why are you looking now?
- Why this company and this role?
- What scope are you looking to own next?
- What compensation range are you targeting?
- What are your location, work-authorization, and start-date constraints?
- Which other processes are relevant to your timeline?
- What questions do you have for us?

## Hiring Manager and Ownership

- Tell me about the hardest product decision you personally owned.
- Describe a product initiative you took from problem signal to the next post-launch decision.
- Which major initiative did you stop, and what evidence justified stopping it?
- Tell me about a promising idea that failed.
- When did your initial diagnosis turn out to be wrong?
- How do you decide what to own yourself and what to delegate?
- What changed in your operating model after a missed commitment?
- What would your previous manager say you needed to improve?

## Product Discovery and Strategy

- How do you decide whether a signal deserves discovery work?
- Tell me about a strategy you created under material uncertainty.
- How did you choose between customer demand and longer-term product direction?
- Describe a time when you rejected a popular feature request.
- How do you distinguish a product problem from a measurement or process problem?
- What would make you revisit a product thesis?
- How do you connect a roadmap choice to business value and opportunity cost?

## Prioritization and Execution

- Tell me about a roadmap conflict between two important stakeholder groups.
- How did you recover a product initiative that was drifting?
- Describe a dependency you could not control.
- When did you knowingly accept product or operational risk?
- How do you set kill criteria before launch?
- What do you do when the team can deliver only half the planned scope?
- How do you know whether a delivery problem requires process change, scope change, or a people decision?

## Analytics and Experimentation

- Walk me through your real working cycle with a product analyst.
- Which metric was hardest to define correctly, and why?
- Tell me about an experiment whose result changed your product decision.
- What would make an A/B result statistically clean but product-wise misleading?
- How do you select a primary metric and guardrails?
- When is an experiment the wrong evaluation method?
- How do you distinguish novelty, delayed effects, and durable impact?
- What do you do when available data is insufficient for the decision?
- Tell me about a time data contradicted stakeholder intuition.

## Metrics Diagnostic Cases

- Retention fell. What do you clarify before forming solutions?
- Revenue increased while retention declined. How do you evaluate whether the change is healthy?
- A feature has high engagement but no visible business impact. What do you check?
- A mandatory internal tool shows rising activity. How do you determine whether it is actually successful?
- An experiment improves the average metric but hurts one segment. How do you decide?
- A launch hit its primary metric but violated a guardrail. What happens next?

## Leadership and Stakeholders

- Tell me about a conflict with an executive or functional peer.
- Describe a decision you influenced without formal authority.
- When did you fail to align stakeholders?
- Tell me about a strong employee or partner you failed to retain.
- Describe an underperformer you managed.
- When did your leadership behavior create a problem?
- How did you rebuild trust after a mistake?
- Tell me about an organizational or process change that met real resistance.
- What type of person or team is hardest for you to work with?

## Platform and Internal Products

- Who is the customer of an internal platform?
- How do you measure value when adoption is mandatory?
- When should a platform team standardize versus allow local solutions?
- How do you connect platform investment to business outcomes?
- Tell me about platform adoption that did not happen organically.
- How do you balance reliability, enablement, and feature delivery?
- How do you prevent usage metrics from becoming vanity metrics in an internal product?

## LiveOps, Monetization, and Game Products

- How do you design an event calendar under revenue pressure without damaging long-term product health?
- Tell me about a monetization change where delayed effects mattered.
- How do you separate a product feature, an economy mechanic, and a monetization surface?
- How do you prioritize LiveOps content, technical debt, analytics, and core product work when they run in parallel?
- What makes a revenue-positive event unsuccessful?
- How do you choose segments for an offer or event?
- How would you diagnose an eCPM or LTV change before recommending a product action?
- Tell me about a case where a cross-track insight changed the roadmap.

## Product Cases

- Design an onboarding improvement for a product with weak activation.
- Improve a mature product whose profitability is declining.
- A core vendor will become unavailable in 30 days. Build the product response.
- Design a gamification mechanic for a non-game product.
- Create a launch and measurement plan for a new recurring feature.
- Decide whether to build, buy, or partner for a critical capability.
- Design a product response to a tenfold scale increase with fixed team capacity.

For every case, allow the candidate to clarify:

- objective and decision owner;
- user or customer segment;
- current baseline;
- time horizon;
- constraints;
- available data;
- acceptable risk;
- definition of success.

## Follow-Up Probes

Use only the probe that exposes a material gap:

- What exactly was your decision?
- What was outside your authority?
- Which alternatives did you reject and why?
- What did you believe before seeing the data?
- What evidence changed your mind?
- How was the metric defined?
- Which segment drove the result?
- What was the counterfactual?
- What did the team disagree about?
- What did you do after the result?
- What would you do differently now?
- Which part of that answer is still an assumption?

## Adaptive Selection

After each answer:

1. Mark the competencies demonstrated.
2. Mark the weakest observable dimension.
3. Decide whether a follow-up can resolve the gap.
4. If yes, probe the same example once or twice.
5. If no, move to a new question that tests the gap through a different example.
6. Avoid using one career story for more than two unrelated competencies.
7. End with the highest-risk unresolved competency, then run one final integrative question.
