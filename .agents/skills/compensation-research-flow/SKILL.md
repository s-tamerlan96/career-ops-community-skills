# Compensation Research Flow

Build a compensation view that separates employer evidence, market observations, the candidate's ask, and the candidate's private acceptance boundary. The skill contains methodology, not current salary data or tax advice.

For Russian IT research, read `references/russian-source-audit.md` as a checklist for evaluating sources. It contains no cached employer bands. Verify source coverage, terms, methodology, and unstable factual claims during the current task.

## Sources and boundaries

- Label material claims `confirmed`, `self-reported`, `inferred`, or `unknown`.
- A written offer or contract confirms what that document says after inspection. A user-relayed recruiter statement is a candidate-reported employer claim until written or directly inspectable.
- Read personal targets only from current user statements or designated private career files such as `config/profile.yml` and `modes/_profile.md`. These files and case records are not shipped with the pack.
- A vacancy, market survey, or anonymous observation cannot establish the candidate's actual compensation or minimum acceptable offer.
- Keep base salary, target bonus, maximum bonus, actual bonus, equity, sign-on, benefits, and total cash distinct.
- Treat discretionary or unverified bonus as upside; do not silently substitute it for a fixed-pay requirement.
- Drafting research or a negotiation script does not authorize outreach, form submission, or hiring-status changes.

## Frame the decision

Record the relevant segment before comparing amounts:

- role family, scope, grade or a scope proxy;
- employer or legal entity if relevant;
- geography and work format;
- employment or contractor arrangement;
- currency, gross/net basis, and monthly/annual period;
- bonus terms and eligibility;
- compensation effective date and observation/publication date;
- the decision: market context, offer comparison, or negotiation preparation.

State missing fields. Continue with explicit assumptions when they do not materially change the result; otherwise ask for the minimum required clarification.

## Build a source ladder

1. **Direct employer evidence:** current written offer, contract, inspectable recruiter band, official vacancy, and relevant policy.
2. **Structured aggregates:** a survey or platform with visible role/location filters, comparable-slice count, collection period, component definitions, and method.
3. **Individual reports:** dated employee or offer reports with enough comparable fields. Treat them as self-reported unless verified.
4. **Broad context:** opaque aggregates, commentary, media summaries, and community polls. Do not turn these into a company-role pay band.

Source quality is claim-specific. An official policy can confirm bonus frequency without confirming amount or actual payout. A salary platform's headline sample size does not establish the sample size of the requested role and location.

Search official sources and role-appropriate salary-disclosed vacancies, then structured surveys and self-reports. Use regional and translated role aliases where helpful. Identify the source owner and method before treating differently named collections as independent.

If values are gated, report the access limitation and use legitimate alternatives. Do not reconstruct hidden data from unrelated snippets.

## Audit freshness and comparability

Capture the date that compensation applied, publication date, source collection window, and current access date. If the event date is absent, publication date is only a weaker freshness proxy. Reconfirm employer terms in the current hiring process; do not treat archived pages as live vacancies or policies.

Compare role scope, grade, location, work format, arrangement, currency, basis, period, components, and date before counting observations. Keep mismatched records separate. Explain voluntary-response bias, self-selection, undercoverage, unverified grades, duplicates, and missing fields.

Deduplicate cross-posts and repeated observations where possible. Subscribers, posts, page views, or all-platform respondents are not comparable-slice observations. Small or unverified samples support individual examples or a descriptive cluster, not a representative market range. Do not claim that a fixed minimum count alone removes these limitations.

## Normalize transparently

- Convert monthly fixed pay to annual fixed only when the paid period and number of payments are established.
- Keep gross and net separate until a verified conversion is possible.
- Record exchange-rate source, date, and direction for currency conversions.
- For contractors, show unpaid time, taxes, contributions, transaction fees, insurance, and benefits separately. Include only supported costs, and distinguish employer-covered charges from costs the candidate bears.
- Show guaranteed compensation separately from conditional scenarios such as no bonus, target payout, and maximum payout.
- Do not annualize a free-text bonus range as expected cash unless its basis is known. Label any illustrative calculation and assumptions.

Before a tax calculation, establish jurisdiction, payment year, tax residency, employment model, income period, other relevant income, deductions, and bonus treatment. Verify current official rules. If critical inputs are missing, retain gross as primary and identify the unresolved inputs rather than inventing a net result.

## Evaluate bonus evidence

Confidence attaches to each claim:

| Evidence | What it supports |
|---|---|
| Written formula and eligibility | Contractual or policy terms as written |
| Verified payout records | Actual outcomes for the recorded periods and population |
| Current policy plus recruiter explanation | Current stated mechanics, with remaining interpretation gaps |
| Comparable independent employee reports | Directional triangulation with self-report limits |
| One anonymous report or “up to” wording | A low-confidence signal and questions to resolve |
| Generic “bonus available” | No numeric conclusion |

Clarify the calculation base, target versus cap, frequency, company/team/personal components, eligibility, proration, start-date conditions, leaver treatment, and actual payout history. Frequency agreement does not verify payout size. Target, maximum, and actual payout remain separate even when sources use the same word “bonus.”

## Produce an auditable result

Use a compact evidence table:

| Claim | Value and basis | Comparable segment | Event / publication date | Source and slice count | Status | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Link to inspected public evidence when appropriate. Keep private offer documents, acceptance floors, and sensitive source links inside the user's private workspace.

Separate confirmed facts, self-reported observations, calculations, and unknowns. Show guaranteed fixed compensation, bonus scenarios, benefits, and contractor adjustments without collapsing them into a misleading single amount.

Name an evidence-supported negotiation range only when the scope and sources support one. If the user wants to state a number and supplies a current expectation, label it a personal ask. If scope or employer band is unknown, prepare questions to resolve them. Never manufacture a market number merely to fill an answer template.

Save a report only where the user requested or in an agreed private user-layer location. End with the decision the evidence supports and the specific remaining questions.
